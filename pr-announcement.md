### Core Protocol Innovations

Custos Protocol operates on a trust-minimized, dual-action escrow model:

1. **Instant Signing Bonus / Upfront Split:** Clients can allocate a portion of the agreement upfront, which transfers directly and immediately to the contractor at contract inception.
2. **True On-Chain Escrow:** The remaining milestone funds are locked directly in the verified Custos smart contract, eliminating reliance on centralized custodians or single-point-of-failure intermediaries.
3. **Anti-Stalling Auto-Release:** Once a freelancer marks deliverables as completed, a cryptographic approval window opens. If the client fails to act or goes silent, funds automatically release to the contributor, permanently eliminating client ghosting.
4. **Arbiter-Free Mutual Dispute Resolution:** In the event of contested deliverables, either party can submit proposed settlement splits. When proposals match, funds distribute immediately according to the mutual agreement, removing arbitrary or corruptible centralized arbiters.
5. **Client Abandonment Recovery:** If a contractor fails to deliver work by the agreed delivery deadline, clients can reclaim the locked funds without delay.
